Urban flows, demographics, visitor interests and microclimate usually
live in four different tools, which is why nobody looks at them together.
City Rhythm puts them on one map and one clock, so that "the square is
crowded" and "the square is 38 °C" can finally be the same sentence.

Presented at SBE25 WestMED in Seville and published in IOP Conference
Series: Earth and Environmental Science.
