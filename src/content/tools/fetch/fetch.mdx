Framework for Environmental Type Classification Hub. LCZ classification
is the standard way to say what kind of climate a piece of city has, and
doing it by hand on a historic centre takes weeks and produces a map
nobody can reproduce.

FETCH combines Google Solar API data with geospatial processing to derive
the urban morphology parameters — sky view factor, building surface
fraction, height of roughness elements — and assigns the zone. The method
behind it was published in *The Sustainable City XVII*.
