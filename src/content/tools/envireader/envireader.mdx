ENVI-met writes its results in a binary pair — EDX for the header, EDT
for the grid — that only its own post-processor reads comfortably. After
a few hundred simulations that becomes the bottleneck of the whole
workflow.

EnviReader parses both in the browser, with no install and no upload, and
produces sections, time series and comparisons between scenarios.
