A suite of climate analysis and outdoor comfort components built on top
of Ladybug Tools: shadow studies, UTCI, urban heat island, weather data
handling.

Ladybug is powerful and steep. These wrappers hide the plumbing so that a
second-year student can run a defensible comfort analysis on a real
square — which is the whole point of teaching the subject at all.
