A thermal camera produces a picture that looks like data and is not: the
colours depend on the palette, on the scale, and on the emissivity the
camera assumed. Warmish opens the raw file, extracts the radiometric
matrix and lets you interrogate it pixel by pixel — set your own
parameters, read the metadata, export the numbers.

Built for field campaigns on urban surfaces, where the question is never
"is it red" but "how many degrees, and against what reference".
