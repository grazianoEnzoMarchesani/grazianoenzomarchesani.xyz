Parametric models know what each surface *is* — a wall, a canopy, a water
body — right up to the moment they are baked, when everything becomes an
anonymous brep.

semanticGeometry attaches custom attributes to nested geometry and carries
them into Rhino, so that the material, the albedo or the LCZ class
survives into the next step of the workflow. Which is exactly what an
environmental simulation needs to read.
