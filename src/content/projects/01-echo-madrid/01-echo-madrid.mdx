A social housing model for the regeneration of Nuevo Mahou-Calderón,
developed for the international workshop RE-LIVE 2025 (SITdA / ETSAM).
Performance-based method throughout: microclimate and CFD simulation,
2050 climate scenarios, passive and circular design strategies, and
social survey tools aimed at containing the risk of green gentrification
— because a cooler neighbourhood that its residents can no longer afford
is not an adaptation success.
