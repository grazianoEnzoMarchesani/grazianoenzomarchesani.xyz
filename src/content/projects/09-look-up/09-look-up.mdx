A video mapping projected on the abbey for UNICAM *La Scienza in Festa*.
Ten years of contests and installations — Giulianova, Osimo, Amandola,
Teramo, Senigallia — sit behind this one: the part of the work where the
building becomes the screen and the audience is the whole square.
