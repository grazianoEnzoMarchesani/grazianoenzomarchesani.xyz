Urban regeneration and climate adaptation of the “school street” on Via
Milano, with architects Stefano Mugnoz and Timothy Brownlee. The
microclimate evaluation report was built on CFD modelling and advanced
ENVI-met simulation: comparative scenarios, 2050 climate projections, the
measured effect of de-paving and urban forestation, and the quantified
performance in comfort terms.

Numbers, so that the choice between asphalt and canopy stops being a
matter of taste.
